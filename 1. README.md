## 4. NIST RMF
- Prepare: Set up the organization to manage risk, including roles, strategy, and common controls.
- Categorize: Determine the impact level of the system and the information it holds.
- Select: Choose and tailor the baseline control set for that impact level.
- Implement: Deploy the selected controls and document how they are implemented.
- Assess: Test whether the deployed controls are implemented correctly and operating as intended.
- Authorize: A senior official reviews residual risk and formally accepts it so the system may operate.
- Monitor: Track the system, its controls, and the threat picture on an ongoing basis.

## 5. Risk Appetite Statement Template
Defines qualitative and quantitative boundaries for acceptable risk.  

Includes examples of EWIs (Early Warning Indicators) and hard limits.
This defines the acceptable levels of risk Havana is willing to tolerate to achieve objectives.

### A. Introduction
Risk appetite guides decision-making, resource allocation, control maturity, and escalation thresholds.

Appetite is the level of risk the organization is willing to pursue, stated in broad terms.

Tolerance is the measurable threshold at which a specific risk becomes unacceptable.
 

Applies across:

- operational risk  
- cybersecurity risk  
- financial crime  
- third-party risk  
- data governance  
- regulatory compliance

### B. Risk Appetite Levels
#### Zero / No Appetite
- Fraud  
- Bribery & corruption  
- Data breaches of sensitive information  
- Non-compliance with laws and regulations

#### Low Appetite
- Operational downtime  
- Third-party outages  
- System failures  

#### Moderate Appetite
- Innovation-related risk  
- Technology change risk  
- Controlled levels of operational error  

#### High Appetite
- Strategic investment risk  
- Growth-related decisions  
- Market expansion initiatives

## 6.Risk Assessment

### 6a. Quantitative Assessment
- SLE: Single Loss Expectancy equals Asset Value multiplied by Exposure Factor.
- ALE: Annualized Loss Expectancy equals SLE multiplied by the Annual Rate of Occurrence.
	
Recorded SLE and ALE are entered by the assessor and are not independently recalculated.	


### 6b. Qualitative Assessment 
Likelihood × Impact scoring matrix used for risk evaluation. Each rated 1 to 5. Risk Score equals Likelihood multiplied by Impact. 

- 1 to 4 is Low
- 5 to 9 is Medium
- 10 to 15 is High
- 16 to 25 is Critical
